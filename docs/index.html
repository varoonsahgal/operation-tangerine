🎯 What you're up against

The exam isn't multiple choice. It's a hands-on exam lab in a live, AWS-provisioned account, where you repair a broken AI-powered customer-intelligence agent.

📋 The format

  • 140 minutes on the clock once you begin. Complete every challenge in that time.
  • A simulated business scenario and a live AWS environment, not a sandbox quiz.
  • Procedural guidance, but no hints. You decide how to diagnose and fix.
  • Intermediate level · Database domain · Free on AWS Skill Builder · Credly badge on passing.
From the exam page itself If something looks broken, re-read the challenge guidance. Most issues come from a missed configuration step, not a platform fault. Read all the guidance before you touch anything.
