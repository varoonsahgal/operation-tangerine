# Product Guide: Headphones and Speakers

## Pairing
Hold the power button for 5 seconds until the light flashes blue, then select the device in Bluetooth settings.
Ultra and Pro models support multipoint pairing with two devices at once.

## Firmware
Firmware updates ship through the Citrus Audio app. Version 4.2 fixes the "left earcup crackle" issue on Pro Headphones.

## Battery
- Headphones: 30 hours (Max line: 45 hours).
- Speakers: 12 hours (Turbo Speaker: 20 hours).

## Common support resolutions
Crackling audio on Pro Headphones is fixed by firmware 4.2. If it persists after updating, offer a warranty replacement.
