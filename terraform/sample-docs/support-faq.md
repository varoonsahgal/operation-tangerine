# Support FAQ (internal)

**Can I change a shipping address after an order ships?**
No. Once status is `shipped`, ask the customer to use the carrier's redirect service.

**A customer was charged twice. What do I do?**
Duplicate charges are usually a pending authorization that drops off in 3-5 business days. If both charges settle, refund the duplicate and log ticket type `BILLING-DUP`.

**How do I escalate an angry customer?**
Set the session state to `escalated`, create an escalation ticket (format `TNG-` plus four digits), and hand off to a supervisor within 1 hour.

**Can enterprise customers pay by card?**
Enterprise customers are invoiced net-30 by default; card payments need finance approval.

**What is Citrus Care+?**
An add-on protection plan covering accidental damage for 2 years. It costs 12% of the item price.
