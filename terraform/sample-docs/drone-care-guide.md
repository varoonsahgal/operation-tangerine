# Product Guide: Drones (Turbo, Nano and Max drone lines)

## Before first flight
- Charge the battery fully (about 90 minutes). The LED turns solid green.
- Calibrate the compass away from metal objects.
- Register the drone with your local aviation authority if it weighs more than 250 g.

## Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Drone drifts left | Recalibrate the IMU on a flat surface |
| Battery drains in under 5 minutes | The battery has degraded; batteries are covered for 6 months |
| Controller won't pair | Hold the power button for 8 seconds to reset pairing |

## Returns
Drones flown more than 10 times can't be returned, but warranty claims still apply (see the warranty policy).
