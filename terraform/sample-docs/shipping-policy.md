# Citrus & Co. Shipping Policy

## Shipping speeds
- **Standard** (5-7 business days): free on orders over $50, otherwise $6.99.
- **Express** (2 business days): $14.99. Free for Zest Club members.
- **Next day**: $24.99, order before 2 PM local time. Not available in the latam region.

## Regions
We ship to us-east, us-west, eu, apac and latam customer regions.
EU orders include VAT at checkout. APAC orders over $1,000 may require customs paperwork, which adds 1-3 days.

## Order statuses
- `processing` - payment captured, not yet picked.
- `shipped` - handed to the carrier; a tracking number is emailed.
- `delivered` - carrier confirmed delivery.

## Lost packages
If tracking has not updated for **10 days**, support may reship the order once at no cost.
A second reship requires a supervisor approval code.
