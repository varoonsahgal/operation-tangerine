# Price Match Policy

Citrus & Co. matches the price of identical items from major retailers within **14 days** of purchase.

- The item must have the same SKU or manufacturer part number and be in stock at the competitor.
- Marketplace sellers, auctions and "lightning deals" are excluded.
- Price matches are refunded as the difference to the original payment method.
- Limit: 3 price-match claims per customer per calendar month.

Zest Club Tangerine members can claim price matches for **30 days** after purchase.
