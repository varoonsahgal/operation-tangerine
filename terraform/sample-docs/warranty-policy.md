# Citrus & Co. Warranty Policy

## Coverage by category
| Category | Warranty |
|---|---|
| electronics | 2 years, parts and labor |
| audio | 2 years, parts and labor |
| kitchen | 1 year |
| office | 1 year |
| fitness | 1 year, frame lifetime |
| outdoor | 1 year (tents: 3 years on poles and seams) |
| apparel | 90 days against manufacturing defects |
| home | 1 year |

## Brand-specific extensions
- **Stark** electronics carry an extra 12 months (3 years total).
- **Wonka** kitchen appliances carry a 5-year motor warranty on blenders.

## How to file a claim
1. Find the order ID in the customer's profile.
2. Confirm the purchase date is inside the warranty period.
3. Open a warranty ticket with code `WTY-` followed by the order ID (for example `WTY-48213`).
4. Warranty replacements ship with free 2-day shipping.

Accidental damage is not covered unless the customer purchased Citrus Care+.
