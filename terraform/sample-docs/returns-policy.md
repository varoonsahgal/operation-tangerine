# Citrus & Co. Returns Policy (v2026.3)

## Standard return window
Customers may return most items within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.
Items must be unused and in original packaging. Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the item is received at the returns center.

## Zest Club members
Zest Club loyalty members get an extended **45-day** return window and free return shipping labels.

## Non-returnable items
- Opened personal-care items
- Gift cards
- Clearance items marked "Final Sale"
- Drones that have been flown more than 10 times (flight log is checked)

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged, the customer must report it within **7 days** of delivery with a photo.
Support agents may issue an instant replacement for orders under $250 without a return.
Orders of $250 or more require a return authorization (RMA) number before a replacement ships.

## Return statuses
`returned` in the orders table means the refund has been processed.
